TITLE:
Tips for Getting Around Campus and Boulder with Ease

SOURCE:
CU Boulder Today
https://www.colorado.edu/today/2025/08/20/tips-getting-around-campus-and-boulder-ease

CONTENT:
CU Boulder offers a variety of transportation options that help students travel around campus and throughout Boulder. Students are encouraged to consider sustainable transportation methods such as biking, public transit, walking, and shared transportation services.

Bikes and E-Bikes

Boulder has an extensive network of bike paths and trails, making cycling one of the easiest ways to travel around the city and campus.

Students can:
- Register their bikes with the Environmental Center.
- Access free bike maintenance assistance.
- Receive help locating stolen bikes through registration records.
- Exchange cable locks for more secure U-locks.

Boulder BCycle

Students receive access to Boulder BCycle, an electric bike-sharing program.

Benefits include:
- Convenient transportation throughout Boulder.
- Electric bicycles that make travel easier and faster.
- Multiple bike pickup and drop-off locations.

Students who do not know how to ride a bicycle can schedule lessons through the Environmental Center.

Skateboards and Scooters

Skateboards and scooters are popular transportation options on campus.

Lime e-scooters:
- Are available throughout Boulder and campus.
- Can be accessed through the Lime mobile app.
- Have speed and location restrictions in certain areas.

Human-powered skateboards are generally allowed on residential streets, sidewalks, bike lanes, and multi-use paths. Storage racks are available near some campus buildings.

Bus Transportation

Buff Buses provide free transportation around campus.

Buff Bus routes connect:
- Central Campus
- Williams Village
- Bear Creek
- East Campus

No pass is required to ride Buff Buses.

RTD Transportation

A Buff OneCard provides access to Regional Transportation District (RTD) bus and rail services.

RTD routes include:
- Transportation throughout the Denver metro area.
- Service to Denver International Airport.
- Connections to local Boulder routes.

Transit App

Students can use the Transit app to:
- Track Buff Buses in real time.
- Track RTD buses and local transit services.
- Find BCycle bike locations.
- Receive route alerts and notifications.

Winter Transportation

CU Ski Bus provides transportation from Boulder to ski resorts in Colorado's mountain corridor, offering students a convenient option for winter recreation.

Motorcycles, Mopeds, and Scooters

Motorcycles, mopeds, and sit-down scooters may operate on city streets but are not permitted on campus sidewalks or pathways.

Vehicles parked on campus must comply with university parking regulations and may require permits.

Carsharing

Students without personal vehicles can use carsharing services.

Available programs include:
- Colorado CarShare
- Zipcar

Students may qualify for discounted memberships and reduced fees through university partnerships.

CU NightRide

CU NightRide is a student-operated transportation service that provides free nighttime rides within Boulder city limits.

Features:
- Safe evening transportation.
- Service for students traveling on or off campus.
- Ride requests available through the CU NightRide mobile app.

Personal Vehicles and Parking

Students who bring vehicles to campus can:
- Purchase parking permits.
- Use daily parking options.
- Access short-term visitor parking areas.

Parking availability may be limited, and transportation alternatives are often encouraged.

Campus Navigation

CU Boulder provides an online campus map that helps students:
- Navigate campus buildings and facilities.
- Find transportation routes.
- Plan efficient travel across campus.

Key Takeaways

CU Boulder offers numerous transportation options including biking, e-bikes, buses, rail transit, scooters, skateboards, carsharing, personal vehicles, and NightRide services. Sustainable transportation methods are strongly encouraged, and many services are available at low cost or no cost to students.
