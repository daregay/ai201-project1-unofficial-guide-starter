TITLE:
CU Boulder Undergraduate Orientation and New Student Guide

SOURCE:
CU Boulder Undergraduate Orientation
https://www.colorado.edu/orientation/undergraduate-students

CONTENT:
The CU Boulder Undergraduate Orientation program helps new students transition to college and prepare for their first semester. New Student & Family Programs serves as a primary resource for incoming students and provides guidance throughout the transition process.

Orientation Requirements
All students beginning their first semester at CU Boulder in Fall 2026 are required to attend orientation during June or July. Orientation allows students to meet other incoming students, become familiar with campus, learn about university resources, and register for their first-semester classes.

Fall Welcome
Fall Welcome takes place during move-in and the first weeks of the semester. Students can attend events designed to help them meet peers, become involved in campus life, and learn about university traditions and resources.

Welcome to Our Herd
Welcome to Our Herd is the university's official welcome event. Students hear from university leaders, participate in college recognition activities, and receive their CU pin.

College Day
College Day provides opportunities for students to meet faculty, staff, and classmates within their academic programs. Students learn about academic expectations, campus resources, co-curricular opportunities, and strategies for success.

International Student Orientation
International students participate in a separate orientation focused on immigration requirements, academic expectations, U.S. classroom culture, and adapting to life in Boulder.

Important Dates
- May: Online Experience and placement assessments become available.
- June and July: New Student Orientation and Transfer Student Orientation.
- August: Move-in, Fall Welcome events, orientation programs, and the start of classes.
- September: Health insurance and student health requirements are due.

Getting Started at CU Boulder
New students should:
- Activate their IdentiKey account.
- Access and regularly check their CU Boulder email.
- Complete the New Student Survey.
- Apply for financial aid and scholarships if needed.
- Review housing options.
- Register for orientation.
- Complete the Online Experience before orientation.
- Submit a Buff OneCard photo.
- Check holds and to-do items in the Buff Portal.

Academic Advising and Registration
Students meet with academic advisors during orientation. Advisors help students understand degree requirements, transfer credits, AP or IB credits, and course selection. Students register for classes during orientation after completing required online modules and placement assessments.

Housing and Campus Life
First-year students can apply for campus housing and explore residence hall options. Transfer students can use off-campus housing resources to find rental opportunities near campus.

Health and Administrative Requirements
Students must complete required health insurance, immunization, and health questionnaire requirements before beginning classes. Final transcripts must also be submitted to the university.

International Student Requirements
International students must follow immigration requirements, arrive before program start dates, complete immigration check-in procedures, and attend International Student Orientation.

Campus Resources
Students have access to numerous support services and resources including:
- Academic advising
- Tutoring services
- Disability services
- Health and wellness services
- Housing and dining services
- Transportation resources
- Veteran and military affairs services
- Student involvement opportunities

Key Message
The orientation process is designed to help students successfully transition to university life by connecting them with campus resources, academic support, university traditions, and fellow students before classes begin.
