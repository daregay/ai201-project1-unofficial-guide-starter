TITLE:
Exploring Boulder: Tips for New Students

SOURCE:
CU Boulder Student Life
https://www.colorado.edu/studentlife/exploring-boulder

CONTENT:
Boulder is located at the base of the Rocky Mountains and offers a combination of outdoor recreation, student life, dining, shopping, and entertainment. New students are encouraged to explore the city and become familiar with the local community before the start of the semester.

Food and Entertainment

The Hill
University Hill, commonly known as "The Hill," is a popular student area located near the University of Colorado Boulder campus. The neighborhood contains restaurants, shops, and gathering spaces frequently visited by students.

Pearl Street
Pearl Street is located in downtown Boulder and serves as one of the city's main destinations for dining, shopping, entertainment, and community events.

Restaurants
Boulder offers a wide variety of dining options, including affordable restaurants and student-friendly food choices throughout the city.

Climate and Elevation

Boulder experiences all four seasons and has a dry climate with frequent sunshine throughout the year.

Students are encouraged to:
- Use sunscreen and sunglasses regularly.
- Carry a reusable water bottle.
- Stay hydrated due to the dry climate and higher elevation.
- Prepare for winter weather, including snow and freezing temperatures.

Winter conditions may require:
- Warm clothing.
- Winter boots.
- A winter coat.
- Gloves, hats, and scarves.

Students who are new to higher elevations should gradually increase physical activity levels while adjusting to the altitude. Staying hydrated can help reduce the risk of altitude-related illness.

Transportation and Getting Around

Boulder is known for being bike-friendly and pedestrian-friendly.

Boulder Creek Path
The Boulder Creek Path provides routes for walking and biking throughout the city while minimizing interaction with vehicle traffic.

Cycling Options
Students can:
- Bring their own bicycles.
- Rent bicycles through the Environmental Center.
- Use Boulder BCycle bike-sharing services.

Students are encouraged to take precautions against bicycle theft and properly secure their bikes.

CU NightRide
CU NightRide is a student-operated transportation service that provides free evening rides within Boulder city limits. The service helps students travel safely around the city at night.

Outdoor Recreation

Boulder offers many outdoor recreation opportunities, including:
- Hiking
- Climbing
- Camping
- Backpacking
- Skiing

Students interested in outdoor activities can explore nearby trails, parks, and mountain destinations.

Outdoor Pursuits
The CU Recreation Center's Outdoor Pursuits program allows students to:
- Rent outdoor equipment.
- Participate in organized trips.
- Learn outdoor skills.
- Explore Colorado with other students.

Skiing and Snow Sports

Boulder is located near several ski resorts.

CU Ski Bus
The CU Ski Bus provides transportation between Boulder and ski resorts along the I-70 mountain corridor, offering students an affordable transportation option during ski season.

Key Takeaways

Boulder provides students with access to outdoor recreation, transportation options, dining, shopping, and entertainment. Students are encouraged to explore the city, stay hydrated while adjusting to the elevation, take advantage of transportation services, and participate in outdoor activities and campus programs to become involved in the Boulder community.
