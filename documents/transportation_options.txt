TITLE:
Transportation Options at CU Boulder

SOURCE:
CU Boulder Parking and Transportation Services
https://www.colorado.edu/pts/transportation-options

CONTENT:
CU Boulder encourages students, faculty, and staff to explore sustainable transportation options when commuting to and around campus. Available transportation methods include public transit, campus shuttle services, biking, walking, carpooling, rideshare programs, scooters, skateboards, and vanpools.

Bus Transportation

CU Boulder students have access to multiple bus transportation services.

Available options include:
- Buff Buses
- City of Boulder HOP service
- Regional Transportation District (RTD) buses

These services provide transportation both on campus and throughout the Boulder area, making it easier to travel without a personal vehicle.

Buff Ride

Buff Ride is CU Boulder’s app-based, on-demand transportation service.

Features:
- Connects East Campus and Main Campus.
- Provides transportation between key university locations.
- Available through a mobile app.
- Designed to improve campus mobility.

Bicycling

Biking is one of the most popular transportation methods in Boulder.

Benefits include:
- Environmentally friendly transportation.
- Reduced transportation costs.
- Physical exercise and health benefits.
- Access to Boulder’s extensive bike infrastructure.

Students can use bicycles for commuting both on and off campus.

Cars and Sustainable Commuting

CU Boulder supports alternatives to single-occupancy vehicle commuting.

Available options include:
- Carpooling
- CarShare programs
- Sustainable commuting initiatives

These programs help reduce traffic congestion and environmental impacts while providing transportation flexibility.

CU NightRide

CU NightRide is a student-operated safe transportation service.

Key features:
- Free evening transportation.
- Available within Boulder city limits.
- Serves students, faculty, and staff.
- Provides safe transportation between campus and off-campus locations.

Transportation Safety

Transportation safety is a university priority.

Programs focus on:
- Bicycle safety
- Pedestrian safety
- Vehicle safety
- Education and awareness initiatives

The goal is to create a safe transportation environment for all campus community members.

Scooters and Skateboards

Scooters and skateboards are commonly used on campus as convenient and environmentally friendly transportation options.

Benefits include:
- Quick travel between campus locations.
- Reduced dependence on motor vehicles.
- Sustainable transportation.

ADA Accessibility

CU Boulder provides transportation resources and services that support accessibility needs for members of the campus community.

Accessible transportation options are available to help individuals travel to and around campus.

Guaranteed Ride Home

CU Boulder offers a Guaranteed Ride Home program for faculty and staff who use sustainable transportation methods.

The program provides transportation assistance during:
- Emergencies
- Unexpected schedule changes
- Situations where normal commuting options are unavailable

Vanpools

CU Boulder supports vanpool programs for employees commuting to campus.

Benefits may include:
- Transportation cost savings
- Free parking
- University subsidies
- Reduced traffic and environmental impact

Key Takeaways

CU Boulder promotes a variety of transportation options designed to reduce traffic, support sustainability, improve accessibility, and provide safe travel throughout Boulder. Students can choose from buses, biking, walking, Buff Ride, NightRide, scooters, skateboards, and other transportation programs depending on their needs.
