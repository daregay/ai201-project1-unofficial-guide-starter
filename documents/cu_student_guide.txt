CONTENT:
Welcome to Boulder and the University of Colorado community. This guide introduces new students to some of the most popular places, activities, and experiences available in Boulder. Students are encouraged to explore the city, enjoy the outdoors, participate in community activities, and take advantage of opportunities both on and off campus.

Top Things to Do and See in Boulder

Pearl Street
Pearl Street is Boulder’s central pedestrian district and a popular gathering place for shopping, dining, coffee shops, local businesses, and entertainment. It is one of the city's most recognizable destinations and attracts both residents and visitors.

The Flatirons
The Flatirons are Boulder’s iconic sandstone rock formations and one of the city’s most famous natural landmarks. Chautauqua Park provides access to hiking trails, scenic viewpoints, and opportunities for outdoor recreation.

Boulder Dushanbe Teahouse
The Boulder Dushanbe Teahouse was handcrafted by artisans in Boulder’s sister city of Dushanbe, Tajikistan. It is known for its unique architecture, decorative artwork, tea selection, and dining experience.

Celestial Seasonings
Celestial Seasonings offers tours that allow visitors to learn about tea production and packaging. Guests can sample teas, visit the Mint Room, and learn about one of the largest tea manufacturers in North America.

Boulder Theater
Boulder Theater is a historic entertainment venue known for concerts, films, and live performances. Its Art Deco architecture and neon marquee are well-known landmarks in downtown Boulder.

Boulder Farmers Market
The Boulder Farmers Market connects visitors with local farmers and food producers. Products include seasonal produce, meats, cheeses, flowers, herbs, and specialty foods from Boulder County.

The Hill
The Hill is a neighborhood located next to the University of Colorado Boulder campus. It is known for student life, restaurants, cafes, music venues, nightlife, and local businesses.

University of Colorado Boulder
The CU Boulder campus contains historic buildings, gathering spaces, and cultural attractions. Popular locations include Old Main, Mary Rippon Theatre, Macky Auditorium, and Fiske Planetarium.

Museum of Boulder
The Museum of Boulder explores the city’s history, culture, and development through exhibits, events, and educational programs. The museum rooftop provides views of the Flatirons.

E-Bike Tours and Cycling
Boulder is known for its bicycle-friendly infrastructure, including bike paths and cycling routes. Visitors can participate in guided e-bike tours or rent bicycles to explore the city.

Outdoor Recreation and Hiking

Boulder offers extensive hiking opportunities throughout the foothills and surrounding open space areas. Popular trails vary in difficulty and provide views of mountains, lakes, canyons, meadows, and rock formations.

Wonderland Lake Loop Trail
An easy hiking trail featuring a scenic lake, views of the Flatirons, and opportunities to observe local wildlife and paragliders.

Flatirons Vista
An easy trail offering panoramic views of the Flatirons mountain range and surrounding landscapes.

Chapman Drive and Tenderfoot Trail
A moderate trail with views of Boulder, nearby forests, and mountain peaks.

Rattlesnake Gulch
A moderate trail in Eldorado Canyon State Park featuring canyon views, historic landmarks, and viewpoints overlooking the Continental Divide.

Mount Sanitas
One of Boulder’s most popular and challenging hikes, known for steep terrain and rewarding summit views.

Green Mountain West Trail
A moderate trail leading to Green Mountain’s summit and providing panoramic views of the surrounding area.

Red Rocks Trail
A short trail near downtown Boulder featuring red sandstone formations and scenic viewpoints.

Flatirons Loop
A moderate-to-difficult trail offering close-up views of the Flatirons and access to Chautauqua’s hiking network.

Visitor and Trail Guidelines

Visitors are encouraged to follow Leave No Trace principles, remain on designated trails, pack out trash and pet waste, and respect natural habitats. Hikers should carry sufficient food and water, prepare for changing weather conditions, and check trail conditions before visiting. Boulder also provides shuttle services to some popular hiking destinations during peak seasons.

Overall, Boulder offers a combination of outdoor recreation, student life, cultural attractions, local food, entertainment, and community experiences that make it a popular destination for University of Colorado students.
